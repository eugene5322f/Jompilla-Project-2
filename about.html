About KCC

A Catholic school in Kabankalan City forming students through faith, education, and service.

  1. Home
  2. About Us

Faith and learning since 1927

KCC began as Kabankalan Institute and grew into a diocesan learning community serving learners in Negros Occidental.

  • Its school motto is "Teach Ye All Nations."
  • Its vision describes a Christ-centered community of accountable, socially responsible people.
  • Its mission emphasizes inclusive Catholic education, research, and meaningful community engagement.

A Brief History

Selected milestones from KCC's published history

Founded as Kabankalan Institute

School beginnings

1927

Local incorporators established a secondary school that later became Kabankalan Private Academy.

First Commencement and War Years

Classes resumed after World War II

1940-1946

The school held its first commencement in 1940, suspended classes during the war, and resumed in school year 1945–1946.

Columban Fathers Acquire the Academy

A new chapter in school leadership

1953

The Columban Fathers, through Fr. Thomas J. Cronin, negotiated to acquire the academy.

College and Elementary Departments Open

Expansion of learning opportunities

1963-1964

A general college and an elementary department opened during the school year.

Diocesan Administration Begins

Leadership transition

1983

After the Sisters of St. Paul's management services ended, school administration shifted to diocesan priests.
