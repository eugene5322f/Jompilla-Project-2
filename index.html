A Catholic learning community since 1927

Kabankalan Catholic College forms students through faith, academic learning, and service in response to the needs of the Diocese and wider community.

  • Founded in Kabankalan in 1927 as Kabankalan Institute.
  • Offers basic education, senior high school, and college programs.
  • Guided by Catholic faith, academic formation, and community engagement.
