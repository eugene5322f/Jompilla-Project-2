Four Steps to Enrollment

Follow the admissions process published by KCC

  1. Submit requirements
    Complete the application form and prepare the required documents.
  2. Entrance examination
    Take the entrance examination to assess academic readiness.
  3. Interview
    Attend an interview with the admissions committee.
  4. Complete enrollment
    Finalize enrollment with the college.

Admission Requirements

Prepare these documents for submission:

  • Completed application form
  • Report card or Form 138
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • PSA birth certificate
  • Four 2x2 ID photos
  • Honorable dismissal for transferees

Enrollment Inquiries

KCC advises applicants to visit the Registrar's Office or contact the college for enrollment details and current availability.
